Anime :

Anime refers to the diverse style of animation created in Japan, characterized by its vibrant artwork,
distinct character designs, and imaginative themes. Unlike cartoons in many Western cultures, which are
often aimed primarily at children, anime spans a massive array of genres tailored to all age groups and
interests—ranging from action-packed shonen adventures and deep psychological thrillers to heartwarming
romances and complex sci-fi epics. Over the decades, it has evolved from a distinct cultural phenomenon
into a massive global industry with millions of fans worldwide, profoundly influencing international pop
culture, fashion, and storytelling.

video-games :

Video games have evolved from simple pixelated pastimes into a dominant global medium that blends art,
storytelling, and technology. They offer immersive experiences where players are no longer just passive
observers, but active participants who shape their own journeys. Across genres—ranging from high-stakes
competitive esports and massive multiplayer worlds to deeply emotional indie narratives—video games foster
global communities and encourage problem-solving, creativity, and collaboration. Today, they stand as a
multi-billion dollar industry that continually pushes the boundaries of entertainment, education, and
digital innovation.
